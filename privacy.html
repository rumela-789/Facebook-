Vimline – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

  • Account: email (login), name, area, username, bio, profile photo. Optional: phone number and a backup Gmail (visible only to you).
  • Content you create: posts, photos, voice notes, comments, reactions, services, reviews, saved items.
  • Messages: direct messages are end-to-end encrypted on your device; we cannot read them. Group messages are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Optional device permissions: microphone and camera (voice/video calls, voice notes), location (nearby services), notifications.

How we use it

To run the app: show your profile and posts to neighbors, deliver messages and calls, send notifications, and keep the community safe (reports, blocks, moderation of adult or abusive content). We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Where it is stored

Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging). Calls are peer-to-peer and encrypted; when a direct connection is impossible they pass through an encrypted relay (TURN) that cannot see the content.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Menu → Settings → Delete account, enter your password. Your profile, posts and services are hidden immediately and everything is permanently deleted after 15 days. Logging in within 15 days restores the account.

If you cannot open the app, email us from your registered address and we will delete the account: YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Children

Vimline is not for children under 13.
